# Sample image

`astronaut.png` is the NASA photograph distributed as `skimage.data.astronaut`
in scikit-image 0.26.0. The image is a 512 × 512 RGB portrait of astronaut
Eileen Collins. The application detects a face region; it does not identify
her or infer anything about her.

Source documentation:
https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.26.x/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut

Original NASA Great Images photograph, as linked by scikit-image:
https://flic.kr/p/r9qvLn

The scikit-image data documentation describes the image as public domain with
no known copyright restrictions. NASA image-use guidance:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

Used only as an educational computer-vision example. No NASA endorsement,
affiliation, or endorsement by the depicted person is implied. The image and
annotated derivative are not relicensed under the application's MIT license.
No scikit-image dependency is required at runtime.
